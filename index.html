The race

RACE°10 is a concept race of ten kilometers. The goal is simple: push ourselves and cross our limits together.

Invitational only. Around thirty runners, one shared target — everyone leaves with a new PB.

It marks the beginning of a new running era: having fun while hitting PBs.

Powered by Le Coureur Flou collective
